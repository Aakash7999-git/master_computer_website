About Yuva Prerna Foundation

Yuva Prerna Foundation

Nawabganj, Gonda, Uttar Pradesh (PIN 271303)

Yuva Prerna Foundation is an active non-governmental organization headquartered in Nawabganj, Gonda, dedicated to holistic community welfare and grassroots development. Founded with the vision of inspiring and mobilizing youth towards positive societal transformation, the foundation focuses on expanding access to practical education, organizing healthcare assistance drives, supporting underprivileged families, and fostering civic environmental responsibility.

Education & Youth Skills Expanding learning opportunities and career skills for rural and semi-urban learners.
Healthcare & Awareness Organizing voluntary medical checkups, eye testing, and preventive health drives.
Social Welfare Providing relief aid, essential rations, and community support to families in need.
Environment & Youth Leading local tree plantation campaigns and encouraging youth volunteerism.
Collaborative Mission

A Shared Vision for a Better Tomorrow

Synergy of Practical Education & Social Welfare

Empowering the next generation with modern tools and civic values

At Master Computer Institute, our primary purpose is delivering quality computer literacy, digital career preparation, and practical office automation training to aspiring youth. When digital literacy is coupled with active community responsibility, it creates lasting social progress.

Through our affiliation with Yuva Prerna Foundation, our students and faculty actively engage with social initiatives, awareness workshops, and community volunteering. This association provides our learners with opportunities to understand social challenges firsthand, participate in constructive service, and use their technical capabilities for the betterment of society.

Clarification of Association: Master Computer Institute operates as an independent technical computer training center in Nawabganj. Our affiliation with Yuva Prerna Foundation is a community and educational outreach partnership; Master Computer Institute is not a government institution, and courses are awarded as independent skill certificates.
Pillars of Service

Core Focus Areas

1. Education & Digital Skills

Empowering learners with fundamental and career-oriented computer education to bridge the digital gap in rural areas.

  • Basic & Advanced Computer Education
  • Office Digital Literacy & Typing
  • Skill Development for Career Readiness
  • Access to Learning Guidance

2. Healthcare & Community Care

Organizing voluntary preventive health drives, awareness sessions, and medical assistance for rural families.

  • Voluntary Health Awareness Camps
  • Vision & Eye Testing Guidance
  • Hygiene & Sanitation Sensitization
  • Village Health Outreach Support

3. Social Welfare & Assistance

Standing with vulnerable individuals and households through direct humanitarian aid and emergency relief.

  • Seasonal Essential Distribution
  • Support for Underprivileged Families
  • Community Emergency Assistance
  • Welfare Information Guidance

4. Youth & Environmental Action

Inspiring young leaders to take ownership of environmental conservation and positive local community progress.

  • Tree Plantation Drives across Gonda
  • Youth Leadership & Discussions
  • Civic Pride & Volunteer Mobilization
  • Environmental Awareness in Schools

ADCA — Advanced Diploma in Computer Applications

Duration: 12 Months | Eligibility: 10th / 12th / All Stream Students

Course Overview

The Advanced Diploma in Computer Applications (ADCA) is a comprehensive one-year program designed to give students in-depth practical expertise in office automation, database management, accounting software, graphic design fundamentals, and workplace digital tools.

  • Complete mastery of MS Office (Word, Advanced Excel, PowerPoint & Access)
  • Computerized accounting and financial reporting with Tally Prime + GST
  • Creative visual designing essentials with Photoshop and CorelDRAW
  • Government exam preparation, typing proficiency, and internet utilities

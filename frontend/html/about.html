About Master Computer Institute

Professional computer learning, modern digital skills, and student-focused education in Nawabganj, UP.

Master Computer Institute is a dedicated computer education center located in Nawabganj, Uttar Pradesh (PIN 271303). Our mission is to make technology and digital skills accessible, practical, and career-oriented for students and learners.

We emphasize hands-on computer practice, foundational computer literacy, software tools, and guided learning to help students build confidence and real-world digital capabilities.

  • Practical, career-oriented computer education
  • Supportive and structured learning environment
  • Located in Nawabganj, Uttar Pradesh (near Tripathi Hospital)
